Hi, I’m Noah. 

I am a post-doctoral researcher with the Oak Ridge Institute for Science and Education (ORISE), working with the United States Forest Service (USFS) Pacific Northwest Research Station. I am currently based in Fort Collins, Colorado. 

My disciplinary focus is Recreation Ecology, examining the social and ecological dimensions of recreation management in parks and protected areas to inform sustainable management strategies. The overarching goal of this research is to balance the opportunities and social benefits of wildland recreation with the habitat and conservation goals of these settings.
