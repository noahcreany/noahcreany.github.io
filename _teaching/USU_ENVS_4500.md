This course is divided into three 5-week sections/modules based on the following
themes:

1) Principles of Wildlands & Recreation
In this first section we will learn what defines wildland, how wildlands are used by humans,
who is using wildlands for recreation, and why people recreate in wildlands.

2) Human and Ecological Dimensions of Recreation Use
The second section of the course will focus on understanding the intersection of social
and ecological issues associated with recreation use of wildlands.

3) Recreation Management Practices & Frameworks
After we have gathered the context of the previous two sections, we will shift focus
to an applied unit where we will examine exactly what is managed in wildlands and
the management frameworks and approaches to provide recreation use and protects ecological resources
