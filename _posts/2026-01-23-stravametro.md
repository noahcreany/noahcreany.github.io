[Creany et al., 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jort.2025.101008)

During my dissertation, I worked with Dr. Christopher Monz to develop methodologies using novel data, such as mobile device data and *Strava Metro*, to estimate recreation use in protected areas. I used *Strava Metro* in my dissertation research to understand behavioral changes in response to trail management actions, and I found it to also be a useful and reliable tool for managers to estimate recreation use in protected areas. 

Here's a share-link for free access to this paper until March 13, 2026: 
[Strava Metro mobility data provides accurate estimates of recreation use in Urban-Proximate Protected Areas](https://authors.elsevier.com/a/1mUVJ7tCXXYbfD)
