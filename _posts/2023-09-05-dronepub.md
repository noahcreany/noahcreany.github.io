[Tomczyk et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jag.2023.103474)

The Recreation Ecology Lab has benefited tremendously from Dr. Monz's international collaborations and partnerships 
which have provided opportunities to work with scholars and researchers from across the world. I have had the pleasure 
of working with and learning from the co-authors of this paper (Aleksandra Tomczyk, Marek Ewertowski, and Francisco Javier Ancin-Murguzur)
about UAVs/Drones, GIS, and remote sensing methods and analysis. I am utilizing UAVs for a chapter in my dissertation and am happy to have 
this article to reference and cite as an example of how new methodological approaches can help inform how we understand and manage
protected area settings for recreation.
