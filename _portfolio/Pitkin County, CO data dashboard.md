I developed this Python-based Shiny dashboard [(link)](https://ncreany.shinyapps.io/rfoc_recreation_use_dashboard/#) as part of a partnership between the USU Recreation Ecology Lab and Pitkin County, Colorado Open Space and Trails (OST) and the Roaring Fork Outdoor Coalition (RFOC).
This dashboard used mobile device data from StreetLight Data to provide estimates of recreation use at nearly 50 sites in Pitkin County and spanning several years.
